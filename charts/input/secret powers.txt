VERSE 3:  	

SECRET POWERS

we're all mortal, that's pretty clear,
with lots to live for, everything to fear
  so I marvel.. at all your secret powers
  I have watched the way you stop time for hours
  you fly over, quietly, with a grace I don't possess..

we're on short time, so we pretend
that our time won't come to an end,
  but I marvel.. at all your secret powers
  you unravel the mystery of moments as they travel
  through your hands, and spin them into gold,
     maybe it's inside us all (if we dig deeper),
     maybe we'll surprise them all, out of nowhere,
     maybe we don't have to fall any farther,
     maybe we could land right here..

I'm sure you have good and bad days,
still I cannot help be amazed,
  and marvel.. at all your secret powers
  as I watch the way you stop time for hours
  you fly over all you love, and it's lovely to behold,
     maybe it's inside us all, if we dig deeper,
     maybe we'll surprise them all, out of nowhere,
     maybe we don't have to fall any farther,
     maybe we could land right here..
                              ..at all your secret powers..
                              ..you stop time for hours,
  you take moments as they pass,
  and spin them into gold..
all your secret powers, all your secret powers...
(you take moments, and spin them into gold...)
